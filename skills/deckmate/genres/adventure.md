## References
- Monkey Island and Thimbleweed Park — study puzzle dependency charts: non-linear puzzle chains designed without dead ends or bottlenecks ([Ron Gilbert](https://grumpygamer.com/puzzle_dependency_charts)).
- Firewatch — study a dialog system that grew from interrupt-heavy barks into long conversations that pause and restart as the player moves ([GDC](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024000/Do-You-Copy-Dialog-System)).
- Oxenfree — study conversations that run while walking, can be interrupted, and are built so the story drives the mechanics ([GDC](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024270/Building-Game-Mechanics-to-Elevate)).
- Return of the Obra Dinn — study deduction as identifying who the dead were from environmental clues, and a 1-bit style kept readable over hours ([Lucas Pope interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/for-lucas-pope-i-return-of-the-obra-dinn-i-was-a-bunch-of-appealing-design-problems)).
- Outer Wilds — study levels that control the pacing and delivery of narrative with knowledge as the only progression ([GDC 2020](https://gdconf.com/news/see-4d-level-design-outer-wilds-deconstructed-gdc-2020)).
## Player communities

- [Adventure Game Studio forums](https://www.adventuregamestudio.co.uk/forums/) — look for puzzle-design threads, dialogue tooling, and postmortems of small adventures.

## Genre pitfalls
- Backwards puzzles, arbitrary solutions, and unclear goals make players feel stupid; Gilbert's rules ask for a clear end objective, obvious sub-goals, and rewarded intent ([source](https://grumpygamer.com/why_adventure_games_suck)).
- Designing puzzles as a flowchart instead of a dependency chart produces dead ends and uneven flow: Maniac Mansion had them, Monkey Island did not ([source](https://grumpygamer.com/puzzle_dependency_charts)).
- Dialogue that stops the game turns talking into a menu; Oxenfree keeps conversation running while the player walks and interrupts ([source](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024270/Building-Game-Mechanics-to-Elevate)).
- A style that reads well in a screenshot can tire over a four-hour game: Pope outlined every shape and reworked dithering for comfort ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/for-lucas-pope-i-return-of-the-obra-dinn-i-was-a-bunch-of-appealing-design-problems)).
