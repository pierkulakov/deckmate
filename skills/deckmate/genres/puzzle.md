## References
- Portal — study chambers that train the button and box before portals, a first portal placed so players see themselves, and a chamber that introduced concepts too quickly ([developer commentary](https://theportalwiki.com/wiki/Portal_developer_commentary)).
- Baba Is You — study levels found by exploring word interactions, and cutting levels whose intended solution needed too many supporting rules ([Hempuli interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-baba-is-you-i-s-delightfully-innovative-rule-writing-system)).
- Opus Magnum — study open-ended puzzles whose toolset is expressive enough that players find optimizations the designers never planned ([GDC](https://gdcvault.com/play/1025715/Open-Ended-Puzzle-Design-at)).
- The Witness and Miegakure — study finding a mechanic's core ideas by following its rules to their consequences instead of inventing puzzles one by one ([IndieCade talk](https://marctenbosch.com/news/?p=181)).

## Genre pitfalls
- Two ideas in one level skip the lesson: Valve notes that Test Chamber 08 still introduced concepts too quickly despite training being a goal from the start ([source](https://theportalwiki.com/wiki/Portal_developer_commentary)).
- Levels that need many active rules at once overwhelm before they teach; Teikari cut setups that were overly specific ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-baba-is-you-i-s-delightfully-innovative-rule-writing-system)).
- An unsolvable state without a cheap way back punishes experimentation: a broken “is you” rule in Baba Is You is one undo away ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-baba-is-you-i-s-delightfully-innovative-rule-writing-system)).
- Designing to one intended optimal solution wastes an open-ended system; Zachtronics does not plan optimal strategies and lets players find them ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-zachtronics-i-opus-magnum-i-)).
- A mechanic chosen for novelty rather than its consequences runs dry; Blow and ten Bosch look for systems whose rules reveal something worth exploring ([source](https://marctenbosch.com/news/?p=181)).
