## References
- Slay the Spire — study a metrics server that records every decision, with pick rates, win rates, and damage taken feeding repeated balance cycles ([GDC](https://gdcvault.com/play/1025731/)).
- Spelunky — study a generator that lays a guaranteed solution path through a 4×4 room grid before filling side rooms, traps, and treasure ([analysis](https://tinysubversions.com/spelunkyGen/)).
- Hades — study knowledge and relationships carried across runs, death as a storytelling device, and a narrative built up during Early Access ([Kasavin interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-with-i-hades-i-creative-director-greg-kasavin)).
- Dead Cells — study hidden assists and control tricks that keep a permadeath game hardcore without rage quits ([GDC](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/video-how-cheating-and-making-player-life-easier-helped-i-dead-cells-i-shine)).
- FTL — study anchoring design to one feeling, captaining a starship, and dropping mechanics that did not serve it ([GDC postmortem](https://gdcvault.com/play/1018034/Designing-Without-a-Pitch-FTL)).

## Genre pitfalls
- Hand-balancing hundreds of cards and interactions is beyond a small team's intuition: Mega Crit built a metrics server and tuned from pick and win rates ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-i-slay-the-spire-i-s-devs-use-data-to-balance-their-roguelike-deck-builder)).
- Random layouts without a guaranteed route produce unwinnable levels; Spelunky builds the solution path first ([source](https://tinysubversions.com/spelunkyGen/)).
- An option with no cost pulls players out of the intended loop: unlimited ammo turned Dead Cells into a kiting game until ammo was reworked ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-watching-people-play-i-dead-cells-i-incorrectly-influenced-its-designers)).
- Permadeath that erases everything also erases the story; Hades keeps knowledge and relationships and treats death as narrative ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-with-i-hades-i-creative-director-greg-kasavin)).
