## First playable
- Hypothesis: different character choices lead to meaningfully different quest outcomes.
- Play one paper encounter with two approaches; record chosen paths and perceived consequences.
- Create Heroes for the first quest and one character build; connect choice to an observable outcome.
- Start with `deckmate project init rpg --data <project>`.
## References
- Disco Elysium — study internal skill interjections and changes to the context of choices while key events remain fixed ([analysis](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/why-i-love-the-interjections-disco-elysium-)).
- Fallout: New Vegas — study choice architecture, build expression, and comparable paths through different skills and traits ([GDC materials](https://ultimacodex.com/2012/03/obsidian-fridays-5-2/)).
- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — study incomplete information, delayed consequences, and the production cost of major branches ([interview](https://nordic.ign.com/cyberpunk-2077/94318/feature/how-the-creators-of-the-witcher-and-cyberpunk-2077-became-the-masters-of-choice-and-consequence)).
- Baldur’s Gate 3 — study world reactivity, permanent quest loss, and failure as a continuation of the story ([interview](https://fandomwire.com/thats-a-consequence-the-baldurs-gate-3-decision-thatll-make-you-miss-out-on-devastatingly-large-amount-of-cutscenes/)).

## Genre pitfalls
- One correct path regardless of build undermines expression — provide comparable solutions across different skills and traits (example: Fallout: New Vegas; [source](https://ultimacodex.com/2012/03/obsidian-fridays-5-2/)).
- Automatically compensating for every lost quest erases the cost of choice — tie real agency to the possibility of losing content (example: Baldur’s Gate 3; [source](https://fandomwire.com/thats-a-consequence-the-baldurs-gate-3-decision-thatll-make-you-miss-out-on-devastatingly-large-amount-of-cutscenes/)).
- Branching without accounting for production resources competes with the rest of the game — budget major branches explicitly before writing them (example: The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt; [source](https://nordic.ign.com/cyberpunk-2077/94318/feature/how-the-creators-of-the-witcher-and-cyberpunk-2077-became-the-masters-of-choice-and-consequence)).
