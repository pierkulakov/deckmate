Sub-genres have their own guides: `rts.md` for real-time strategy, `4x.md` for empire building, and `tactics.md` for turn-based squad combat. Read this file together with the closest of them.
## References
- Civilization IV — study an AI that plays to lose well: a challenge without cheating that breaks role-play and diplomacy ([Soren Johnson, GDC 2008](https://www.designer-notes.com/playing-to-lose-ai-and-civilization-gdc-2008/)).
- Civilization — study games as a series of interesting decisions and why a secondary technology tree was cut ([Sid Meier, GDC 2012](https://gdcvault.com/play/1015756/Interesting)).
- Age of Empires — study design by playing: a rough prototype reshaped through frequent tests with a varied tester base ([Ensemble](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/ensemble-s-shelley-explains-design-by-playing-)).
- Wargroove — study a map editor shared with players, a distinct critical-hit condition for every unit, and naval and air units priced against ground ([Chucklefish interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/an-inside-look-at-i-wargroove-s-i-wicked-design-choices)).
- Frostpunk — study scenarios written from a one-sentence concept, hope and discontent as a balance, and laws that open new dilemmas ([11 bit talk](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/6528/igc-2018-empathize-with-numbers-the-process-of-frostpunk-scenario-writing)).

## Genre pitfalls
- An AI that cheats to be hard breaks the role-play; Johnson separates good AI that plays to win from fun AI that plays to lose ([source](https://www.designer-notes.com/playing-to-lose-ai-and-civilization-gdc-2008/)).
- A decision that is interesting once is not interesting ten times: Meier removed Civilization's secondary technology tree ([source](https://gdcvault.com/play/1015756/Interesting)).
- Testers who share the team's taste hide problems; Shelley asks for a varied test base and decisive use of its feedback ([source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/ensemble-s-shelley-explains-design-by-playing-)).
- Choices without pressure feel optional: Frostpunk's The Arks scenario made laws feel unnecessary until its balance was reworked ([source](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/6528/igc-2018-empathize-with-numbers-the-process-of-frostpunk-scenario-writing)).
